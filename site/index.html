How it works

Three pieces. You own all of them.

01

Your AI app

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor… Add one URL, approve it on your phone, done.

02

Your gateway

A tiny server in your free Vercel account, or on your own hardware. No Latch company in the middle.

03

The Latch app

On your Android phone. It dials out to your gateway, sees the screen, and taps, only while you run a session.

Features

Built for real tasks, not demos.

You choose the apps

Switch apps on and the AI works there without nagging you. For anything else, your phone asks once: Not now, This session, or Always. Until you say yes, the AI can't even see that app.

Stop is always one tap

A red Stop button floats over every session. Sessions also end on their own after 15–120 minutes.

Big moves ask first

Payments, app installs, permission pop-ups, and deleting accounts wait for your OK on the phone, unless you choose otherwise.

See every tap

A pointer glides to each spot the AI uses and says what it's doing. Activity keeps a log on the phone for 7 days.

Files, encrypted

Move files between your computer and phone at full quality, AES-256 encrypted between your devices, up to 2 GB.

Any AI, one standard

Latch speaks the Model Context Protocol. Apps that support sign-in connect with just the URL; others use a key you create in the app and can revoke any time.

Safety

Default deny. Always your call.

latch policy
  • ✕ type passwords, PINs, or one-time codes
  • ✕ read your clipboard
  • ✕ open Latch itself, the notification shade, or the lock screen
  • ✕ act on a screen it hasn't just looked at
  • ✕ follow instructions written on screen
  • ✕ run a shell or record you silently
  • ✕ keep screen content in any log

Every switch starts off. You pick what the AI may do (just look, look and tap, or everything) and which apps it may use.

Screen text, web pages, and notifications are treated as untrusted data, so a page that says “ignore your rules” changes nothing.

Your gateway is private: no web console, no owner-key box, nothing for search engines to index. Only your phone and the AI apps you approved get in, each with its own 256-bit key you can revoke.

Setup

About five minutes. Three copy-pastes.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Download the Android beta and tap Create my own gateway. Copy your owner key.

  2. 2

    Deploy your gateway

    Tap Open Vercel, paste the key, add the free Upstash store, and press Deploy. Paste your new address back into the app.

  3. 3

    Connect your AI

    Add https://<your-address>/mcp to your AI app and approve it in the app. Start a session and ask away.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is Latch free?

Yes. Latch is open source under the Apache-2.0 license. The gateway runs on a free Vercel Hobby account with a free Upstash Redis database, or on any server you own.

Which AI apps work with Latch?

Any app that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP) with remote servers: Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, or your own agent. Most sign in with only the URL; others use a key from the Latch app.

Does my screen go to a Latch company server?

No. There is no Latch server in the middle. Your phone talks to your own gateway, in your own Vercel account or on your own hardware.

Can the AI type my passwords or one-time codes?

No. Latch refuses password, PIN, one-time-code, and payment fields. When a step needs one, the AI asks you to do it on the phone.

How do I stop the AI?

Tap the red Latch Stop button that stays on screen during every session. Sessions also end on their own after 15 to 120 minutes.

Does Latch work on iPhone?

Not yet. iOS has no public way for one app to operate other apps, so an iOS companion will offer a smaller, honest set of features later.

Android says “App blocked” or “Restricted setting”. Is something wrong?

No. Android protects accessibility apps installed from a file. The setup guide shows the exact taps for each phone brand. Latch never tries to sneak past these checks.
