# Latch name and logo policy

The code of Latch is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) with the additional terms in [NOTICE](NOTICE). The **name and logo are not part of that license.**

**Project marks:** the name "Latch" as used for this software, and the Latch logo (a hook that has caught a pin, in any color).

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your project is "based on Latch" or "compatible with Latch", with a link to https://github.com/aspershupadhyay/latch.
- Writing about Latch, reviewing it, or teaching it, and showing the logo to refer to this project.
- Distributing **unmodified** official builds under the Latch name.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Publishing a modified version, fork, app, gateway, or service under the name "Latch" or a confusingly similar name, or with the Latch logo or a look-alike.
- Listing anything in an app store, package registry, or domain name in a way that suggests it is the official Latch or is endorsed by its maintainers.

A fork must use its own name and logo, keep the attribution the [NOTICE](NOTICE) requires, and say that it is modified.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/aspershupadhyay/latch/issues.
